# Teaching samples

Package Manager lists **Rock and Moss**, **Roof and Snow**, and **Wall and Grime** under SimpleBlend's Samples section. Import the desired example, then open its scene under `Assets/Samples/SimpleBlend/<version>/`. See its README for the complete lesson.

Use a URP project with matching Graphics and active Quality render assets. Samples do not install or replace pipeline settings. Each scene has a saved camera, a light, editable per-renderer paint, and a visible caption with channel mapping and brush starting points.

All three use `SimpleBlend/Layer Blend 3`: unpainted is the base, Red is the main overlay, Green is the secondary overlay, while Blue and Alpha are free. Source meshes remain unpainted. Sample assets are saved data; the imported scenes do not depend on development/test authoring methods.

The procedural assets are covered by the package MIT notice. Built-in Unity caption font and URP shader-library dependencies are supplied by Unity, not copied into the sample. No custom scripts, fonts or paid assets are needed.

First-use review should cover paint, erase, smooth, previews, Undo/Redo, scene save/reopen, and independent paint on a duplicate. Automated import checks do not substitute for an owner-run walkthrough or appearance review.
